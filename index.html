Ancient Families

A record of the families of the ancient world.

Ancient Families is a genealogical reference documenting the individuals, families and dynasties of the ancient world.

The database brings together known family relationships, including parents, spouses and children, alongside historical dates and references to published sources.

From kings and queens to lesser-known historical figures, each individual has their own record, providing a place to explore their ancestry, descendants and connections to others.

About

Ancient Families is an ongoing reference project. Information is compiled from historical publications and academic research, with sources provided alongside individual records wherever possible.

Ancient genealogies are often incomplete, uncertain or disputed. Records should therefore be considered in the context of the surviving historical evidence.
